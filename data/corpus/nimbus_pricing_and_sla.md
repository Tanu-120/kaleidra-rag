# Pricing and Service Levels

## Plans

Nimbus offers three plans: Starter, Business and Enterprise. Starter includes one Broker Cluster and 50 gigabytes of retained data. Business adds multi-region replication and a ninety-nine point nine percent uptime SLA. Enterprise includes dedicated Broker Clusters, private networking and a ninety-nine point ninety-five percent uptime SLA.

## Refund Policy

Customers on the Business plan can request a refund within thirty days of purchase. Enterprise plans are billed annually and are refundable pro rata only if Nimbus Labs misses the contractual SLA for two consecutive months. Starter plans are not refundable but can be cancelled at any time.

## Support

Business customers receive support during working hours with a four hour first response time. Enterprise customers receive twenty-four hour support with a thirty minute first response for severity one incidents. Severity one incidents are declared when the Nimbus Gateway is unavailable for more than five minutes.
